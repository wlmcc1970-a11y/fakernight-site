Three games in one app

Add everyone's names, pick a game and pass the phone. Each game has a short How to play, so nobody needs to know the rules first.

Find the Faker

Everyone secretly sees the same word, except the faker. Give one-word clues, then point on three and vote someone out. Caught fakers still get one guess to steal the round.

3 to 12 players

Most Likely To

Read the prompt, everyone points on three, and the phone keeps a running tally. Kind and playful prompts, made for family nights.

3 to 12 players

Forehead Guess

Hold the phone to your forehead while friends act it out or describe it. Tap Got it or Pass before the timer runs out.

2 to 12 players

See it work

Real screens from the app. Swipe sideways to see them all.
