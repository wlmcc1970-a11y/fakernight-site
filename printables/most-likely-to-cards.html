How to play Most Likely To

  • Cut the cards apart along the dashed lines and shuffle them.
  • Read a card out loud: "Who is most likely to..."
  • Count three, two, one, and everyone points at someone at the same time.
  • Whoever gets the most fingers takes the card. No winners or losers, just bragging rights.
