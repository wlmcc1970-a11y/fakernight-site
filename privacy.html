Privacy

Faker Night: Party Games, by DigiRune Studios (McCullough Development, Inc.). Last updated October 8, 2026.

The short version

Faker Night collects nothing about you. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics in the app.

What stays on your phone

Player names, your own word lists, your settings and whether you unlocked the full game are saved only on this phone, so the game works with no signal. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether this store account owns the unlock.

Children

Faker Night does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website has no ads and sets no cookies. It counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that does not collect personal information or follow you to other sites.
